# Head of Growth Playbook

## Funnel model (AARRR)
Acquisition (reach and visits), Activation (first real value), Retention (come back or renew), Revenue (pay and expand), Referral (bring others).
Find the **weakest stage with enough volume to matter** and fix it before buying more traffic.

## Funnel report template
Stage, count, conversion from the previous stage, trend, suspected cause, test planned. Use client numbers only. If a stage is not measured, mark it unknown and propose how to measure it.

## Channel testing method
1. List 5-8 plausible channels for this client's customers (use evidence of where the best customers came from).
2. Pick the 2-3 cheapest to test properly.
3. Run each for a fixed budget and period, with the same offer and a clear metric (cost per qualified lead, cost per customer).
4. Compare, double down on winners, and stop losers. Record results in the experiment log.

## Experiment card
```
ID / name:
Hypothesis: If we <change>, then <metric> will <move by X> because <reason>
Metric and baseline (label):

Approval needed from:
Result (after):

Lesson:
```

## Prioritizing the backlog (ICE)
Score impact, confidence, and ease from 1-10. Priority = average of the three. Revisit confidence after each learning.

## Data cautions
- Too few conversions give noise. As a rough guide, avoid calling a winner below about 30 conversions per variant. State the uncertainty and keep testing.
- Change one thing at a time where possible.
- Check for seasonality and one-off spikes.
- Judge by cost per **qualified** outcome, not clicks.

## Retention and referral ideas to evaluate
Onboarding checklist, first-win milestone, check-in cadence, renewal reminders, win-back messages, loyalty or credit, referral reward matched to margin, case studies from real customers.

## Budget allocation rule of thumb
Keep most budget on proven channels and reserve a smaller slice (often 10-20%) for tests. Label the split as a heuristic and adapt it to the client's cash position.

## Vanity vs real metrics

| Vanity | Real |
|---|---|
| Followers, impressions, page views | Qualified leads, booked meetings, customers, retention, revenue per channel, payback |
