# CFO Playbook

## Financial health snapshot
Revenue, gross margin, operating costs, net profit, cash on hand, monthly burn, runway (cash / monthly net burn), receivables and payables ageing, concentration (top customer share of revenue). Label every number.

## Break-even
- Break-even units = fixed costs / (price per unit - variable cost per unit)
- Break-even revenue = fixed costs / contribution margin %
Show the result alongside current volume.

## Initiative business case template
```
Initiative:
Investment: one-off + recurring (label)
Expected benefit: incremental revenue, gross profit, cost savings (label, with drivers)
Timing: when costs and benefits fall (cash view, by month)
Payback period = investment / monthly incremental gross profit
ROI = (incremental gross profit over the period - total cost) / total cost
Sensitivity: what if benefits are 30% lower, or delayed by 3 months?
Funding source and cash impact at the lowest point:
Verdict (Clear / conditions / Needs change / Block):
```
Use a **cash view** as well as a profit view. Profitable growth can still run out of cash because of payment delays and upfront costs.

## Cash-flow forecast (13-week or 12-month)
Opening cash, cash in (by customer or stream, with realistic payment delays), cash out (fixed, variable, one-offs, tax), closing cash. Build three cases: base, downside (for example lower sales or slower payments), upside. Find the **lowest cash point** and when it occurs.

## Affordability test for a large contract or hire
- Upfront cost before first payment, and the payment terms
- Margin after real delivery cost (COO input)
- Cash low point with and without it
- Concentration risk if it becomes more than about a quarter of revenue (heuristic)
- What happens if the customer pays late or cancels?

## Budget allocation
Rank initiatives by expected profit per unit of cost and by confidence. Fund proven ones first, cap experiments, and keep a cash reserve sized to the client's runway target.

## Financial hygiene checklist
- [ ] Business and personal money separated
- [ ] Invoices sent promptly with clear terms, overdue ones chased
- [ ] Costs categorized monthly
- [ ] Prices checked against current costs
- [ ] Cash forecast updated regularly
- [ ] Taxes and statutory payments calendared (confirm with a qualified accountant)

## Currency and local context
Use the currency in the client profile. When inflation or exchange rates matter, show the sensitivity and cite the source and date of any rate used.
