# Chief of Staff Playbook

## Routing examples

| Request | Chain |
|---|---|
| "How do we get more clients?" | BDM, Head of Growth, Revenue, then COO capacity, Compliance (outreach), CFO (budget) |
| "Should we enter a new market?" | CSO, Strategist, CFO, COO, Compliance, CGO, then owner decision |
| "Our revenue is flat" | Revenue (diagnose), Head of Growth (funnel), BDM (pipeline), CGO (priorities) |
| "Can we take this big contract?" | COO (capacity), CFO (cash and margin), Compliance (terms), BDM (proposal), then owner |
| "Give me a full review" | Run `/growth-review` |

## Decision brief template

```
# Decision Brief: <topic>   (<date>, client: <slug>)

## Bottom line
<3-5 sentences>

## Where the officers agree
- ...

## Where they disagree
| Issue | Position A (officer, evidence) | Position B (officer, evidence) | Trade-off |

## Review verdicts
COO: <verdict> | CFO: <verdict> | Compliance: <verdict>

## Risks
- ...

## Decisions needed from you
1. <Decision>. Recommended: <option>. Alternative: <option>. Deadline: <date>.
```

## Conflict memo template

```
# Conflict Memo: <topic>
Decision to be made:
Option A (<officer>): action, evidence, benefit, cost, risk, labels
Option B (<officer>): action, evidence, benefit, cost, risk, labels
Effects on revenue / profit / customers / operations / long-term strategy:
Chief of Staff's note on what evidence would settle it:
Who decides (from profile approval table):
```

## Decision-log hygiene
- Use IDs like D-001, D-002. One row per decision.
- Always fill a review date (usually 30 days after implementation).
- When a decision is rejected, record the reason. Rejected ideas should not come back unchanged.

## Context isolation checks
- Did the owner mention a different client earlier in this conversation? Ask them to start a new conversation.
- Are all facts traceable to `clients/<slug>/` or a cited source?
