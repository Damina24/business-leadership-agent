# Growth Strategist Playbook

## Where growth can come from (Ansoff-style)
1. Sell more of the current offer to current customers (frequency, basket size, retention)
2. Sell the current offer to new customers (new segments, regions, channels)
3. Sell new offers to current customers (add-ons, adjacent services)
4. New offers to new customers (highest risk, so test small)

## Business model check
- Who is the customer and what job are they hiring the offer to do?
- Why do they choose this client over alternatives (including doing nothing)?
- Where does the model leak: acquisition cost, delivery cost, churn, pricing?
- What proof exists of demand? (paying customers, repeat purchases, waiting lists)

## Value proposition check
Customer job, pain, gain, alternatives, why this client, proof. If "proof" is empty, say so.

## Market sizing
- Prefer **bottom-up**: reachable customers x realistic annual spend x achievable share. Show every input with a label.
- **Top-down** figures only with a cited source, and still sanity-check bottom-up.
- Report a low, base, and high case.

## Competitor matrix
Competitor, offer, price, target customer, strengths, weaknesses, channels, evidence (URL, date). Note gaps the client can own.

## Market-entry checklist
- [ ] Demand evidence: who pays, how much, how often (VERIFIED or flagged)
- [ ] Cost to enter and time to first revenue
- [ ] Competition and the client's differentiation
- [ ] Execution needs: capabilities, licenses, partners, channels
- [ ] Smallest viable test, and kill criteria (when to stop)
- [ ] Compliance and regulatory points to hand to the Risk and Compliance Officer

## Options comparison template

| Option | Expected benefit (label) | Cost | Time to impact | Risk | Return | Confidence | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Always include "do nothing / improve the core" as an option.

## Roadmap template
- 0-30 days: validate and prepare (data, tests, approvals)
- 30-90 days: pilot with success thresholds
- 90-180 days: scale if thresholds are met, or stop

Each step: action, owner, cost, metric, decision point.
