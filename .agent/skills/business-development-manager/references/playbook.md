# BDM Playbook

## Ideal customer profile (ICP)
Define from the client's best existing customers (VERIFIED), not from guesses:
industry, size, location, trigger events (why now), pain, budget range, buyer role, decision process, disqualifiers.

## Qualification (score each 0-2, qualified at 7+ out of 10)
- **Need:** clear problem the client solves
- **Budget:** money exists or can be approved
- **Authority:** talking to, or can reach, the decision-maker
- **Timing:** a reason to act within the client's sales cycle
- **Fit:** matches the ICP and the client can deliver (check with COO)

## Pipeline stages
Identified, Researched, Contacted, Responded, Qualified, Meeting held, Proposal sent, Negotiation, Won or Lost.
Record the **reason** for every Lost or Stalled deal.

## Prospect record (minimum fields)
Organization, contact name and role, source (URL or file) and date found, ICP fit score, stage, last contact, next step and date, notes, consent or opt-out status.

## Outreach principles
- Personalized with a verifiable detail, short, and one clear ask.
- True claims only, with proof the client actually has.
- Always include an easy opt-out.
- Follow-up cadence: a few spaced touches, then stop. Respect "no".
- Draft for approval. Run Compliance for new channels, regions, or bulk outreach.

## Proposal outline
1. Their situation and goal (in their words)
2. Recommended solution and scope
3. Deliverables, timeline, and what the client needs to provide
4. Pricing (as approved by the owner) and terms
5. Proof: relevant results and references (real ones only)
6. Next steps

## Partnership register fields
Partner, type (referral, reseller, co-delivery, supplier), what each side gives and gets, due diligence, risks, owner, status.

## KPI formulas
- Lead-to-customer conversion = customers won / qualified leads
- Acquisition cost = (sales and marketing spend) / new customers in the period
- Sales cycle length = average days from first contact to signed deal
- Pipeline coverage = pipeline value / revenue target (3x is a common rule of thumb, so verify against the client's own win rate)
- Weighted forecast = sum of (deal value x stage win probability), using the client's historical rates when available and labeled ASSUMPTION when not

## Pipeline report template
Stage counts and values, new this period, won, lost with reasons, stalled, forecast (low, base, high), top 5 next actions.
