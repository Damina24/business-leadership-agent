# Risk and Compliance Playbook

**Scope note.** This is a screening framework, not legal advice. Laws differ by country and industry and change over time. Always confirm the current rules for the client's jurisdictions from a primary source, cite it with the date, and send open questions to a qualified professional.

## Checks by activity

### Outreach (email, SMS, calls, social messages)
- Is there a lawful basis or consent for this channel and recipient type (business or consumer)?
- Is it clear who is sending, with a working opt-out and a way to honor it?
- Are suppression and "do not contact" lists kept?
- Rules to check where relevant: GDPR and ePrivacy or PECR (EU, UK), CAN-SPAM and TCPA (US), the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and related regulator guidance, plus local telecom and anti-spam rules. Verify which apply and their current text.

### Marketing and advertising claims
- Every factual claim (results, rankings, savings, "best") has evidence on file.
- Testimonials are real, with permission, and not misleading. Disclose paid endorsements and affiliate links.
- No fake urgency, hidden fees, or "before and after" that is not typical without saying so.
- Check platform ad policies and any industry-specific rules.

### Personal data
- What data, why, and the lawful basis. Collect the minimum.
- Where it is stored, who has access, how long it is kept, how to delete it.
- Cross-border transfers and third-party tools (CRM, email platforms, analytics).
- Breach plan: who is told, and how quickly (regulator and individuals, as required).

### Contracts and partnerships
- Scope, payment terms, liability, termination, IP ownership, confidentiality, exclusivity, data processing terms.
- Authority: who is allowed to sign for the client.
- Anything beyond a standard template goes to a lawyer.

### Regulated or licensed activity
- Does the client need a license, registration, or professional qualification for this offer or market? Check before selling.

### Intellectual property and content
- Use only content and images the client owns or has a license for. Check trademark use and competitor comparisons.

### Reputation and ethics
- Would this look fair if it were public? Does it treat customers, staff, and partners honestly?
