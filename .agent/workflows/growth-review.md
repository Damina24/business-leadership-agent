# /growth-review

Run the full chain for the active client. Use the `chief-of-staff` skill to coordinate. Follow `shared/context-protocol.md` first.

1. **Baseline** (`chief-growth-officer`): current growth targets, KPIs, and trend against the profile and data. Note gaps in the data.
2. **Revenue** (`revenue-growth-manager`): revenue streams, margin, pricing, retention, leakage.
3. **Funnel** (`head-of-growth`): where prospects drop out, channel performance, quick experiments.
4. **Pipeline** (`business-development-manager`): pipeline health, prospect gaps, partnership opportunities.
5. **Capacity** (`chief-operating-officer`): can the organization deliver the growth being considered?
6. **Finance** (`chief-financial-officer`): affordability, cash timing, return on the proposed initiatives.
7. **Compliance** (`risk-compliance-officer`): screen the initiatives that involve outreach, data, claims, or contracts.
8. **Prioritize** (`chief-growth-officer`): score and rank initiatives with the opportunity model. Pick a short list that fits capacity and budget.
9. **Quality gate and brief** (`chief-of-staff`): run `shared/quality-gate.md`, surface disagreements, and produce the decision brief.
10. Save to `clients/<slug>/outputs/reports/YYYY-MM-DD-growth-review.md`, and add the actionable items to the decision log with review dates.

When the owner wants to hand the results to the client, offer `/client-pack` to turn everything into one client-ready PDF.
