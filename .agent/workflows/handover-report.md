# /handover-report

Use the `chief-of-staff` skill. Follow `shared/context-protocol.md`.

1. Read the profile, decision log, and all reports for the active client.
2. Write a handover report with these parts:
   - Engagement scope and what was delivered
   - Goals versus results (KPI, baseline, target, actual)
   - Key decisions and their outcomes, including what failed and why
   - Current state of pipeline, experiments, and processes (what the client can run without us)
   - Open risks and unresolved issues
   - Recommended next 90 days
   - Data and files the client should keep
3. Mark every figure VERIFIED, ESTIMATE, or ASSUMPTION.
4. Save to `clients/<slug>/outputs/reports/YYYY-MM-DD-handover-report.md`.
5. Remind the owner to archive or remove the client folder when the engagement is over, according to the contract and data-retention terms.

When the owner wants to hand the results to the client, offer `/client-pack` to turn everything into one client-ready PDF.
