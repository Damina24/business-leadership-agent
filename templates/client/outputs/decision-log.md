# Decision Log

| ID | Date | Officer(s) | Recommendation | Expected impact (label) | Status | Owner decision / date | Review date | Actual result | Lesson |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

**Status values:** Proposed, Approved, Rejected, Deferred, In progress, Implemented, Abandoned.
