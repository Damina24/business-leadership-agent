# Report Format

Every saved report uses this structure. Keep it scannable: the owner is busy. Aim for 1-3 pages unless the task needs more.

```markdown
---
client: <slug>
date: YYYY-MM-DD
officer: <role(s)>
question: <the question this answers>
data_confidence: High | Medium | Low
---

# <Title>

## 1. Bottom line
Three to five sentences: the recommendation, expected impact, and main risk.

## 2. What we know
Key facts, each with an evidence label (see shared/evidence-standards.md).

## 3. Analysis
Options considered with costs, benefits, risks, expected return, and why the recommended option wins.

## 4. Recommendations
| # | Action | Expected impact (metric, size, label) | Cost / resources | Timeline | Owner | Approval needed from |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## 5. Risks and dependencies

## 6. Reviews
COO capacity check / CFO check / Compliance check: verdict and conditions, or "not required: <reason>".

## 7. Handoffs and open questions

## 8. Sources
```

Each recommendation must state: expected business impact, measurable outcome, timeline, resources, and responsible party (Rules 4 and 5).
