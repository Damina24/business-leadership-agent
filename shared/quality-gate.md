# Quality Gate

The Chief of Staff runs this before presenting any major recommendation or consolidated brief. Fix failures before the owner sees the work.

## Checklist

**Grounding**
- [ ] Based on the active client's profile and data, not on generic advice or another client.
- [ ] Every decision-driving number carries VERIFIED, ESTIMATE, or ASSUMPTION, with source or method.
- [ ] Arithmetic re-checked. Totals and percentages add up.
- [ ] No invented market data, prospects, contacts, or financials.

**Actionability**
- [ ] Each recommendation has an action, expected impact, metric, timeline, resources, and owner.
- [ ] Short-term gains were weighed against long-term viability.
- [ ] Growth was judged on profit and cash, not just revenue or activity.

**Coordination**
- [ ] Earlier reports and decisions were reviewed, so no duplicated work.
- [ ] COO check done if customer commitments are created.
- [ ] CFO check done if spend, pricing, or forecasts are involved.
- [ ] Compliance check done if outreach, data, claims, contracts, or regulated activity are involved.
- [ ] Disagreements between officers are shown, not hidden.

**Authority**
- [ ] No officer committed to pricing, contracts, spend, or hiring. Everything outside delegated authority is marked "needs owner approval".
- [ ] Material risks and major financial commitments are escalated.

**Record**
- [ ] Report saved in `outputs/reports/`.
- [ ] Decision-log rows added, with a review date.

## Output
End the brief with: **Decisions needed from you** (numbered, each with a recommended option and the alternative).
