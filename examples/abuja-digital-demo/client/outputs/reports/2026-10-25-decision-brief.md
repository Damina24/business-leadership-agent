# Decision Brief: First 90 Days (2026-10-25, client: demo-digital-abuja)

## Bottom line
Launch with a Starter Website, turn each client into a monthly Care Plan, and hold off on large custom apps for now. In the base case the company reaches monthly cash break-even in month 9. If sales run at about half that pace, cash runs out around month 8, so a checkpoint on 2027-01-23 decides whether to cut costs or find bridge funding. All company figures are unverified owner estimates.

## Where the officers agree
- Starter Website is the right entry offer. Care Plans are the most profitable use of hours (₦10,500 per hour against ₦5,083 for websites and ₦4,000 for apps).
- Payment must be 50% upfront and 50% on delivery.
- Outreach is drafted by the team and approved and sent by the founder.
- Business Web Apps wait until there is more capacity and cash.

## Where they disagree

| Issue | Position A | Position B | Trade-off |
|---|---|---|---|
| Founding-client discount | **Revenue Growth Manager:** 10% for the first 3 clients (₦35,000 each, ₦105,000 in total) | **Business Development Manager:** 20% (₦70,000 each, ₦210,000 in total) to win the first clients faster | The 20% option costs ₦105,000 more in cash and needs about 30% more volume to break even, while capacity is limited. The 10% option may win fewer first deals. No evidence yet shows which one works. |

**What would settle it:** the reasons the first 3 to 5 prospects give when they accept or decline. **Chief of Staff note:** start at 10%. Move to 20% only for a client who also gives a written testimonial and a referral introduction, and only with the founder's approval.
