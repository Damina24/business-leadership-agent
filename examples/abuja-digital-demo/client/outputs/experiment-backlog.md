# Experiment Backlog (Head of Growth)

Updated 2026-10-25. Monthly growth budget: ₦150,000 (owner-set). Capacity rule from the COO: experiments must not take more than 20 hours of founder selling time in the first 30 days.

| ID | Experiment | Hypothesis | Metric | Cost | Success threshold | Stop threshold | Approval needed | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| E-01 | Free 20-minute digital audit for 10 Abuja businesses | If we offer a specific audit of a business's online presence, at least 3 of 10 will agree to a follow-up conversation | Qualified conversations, then signed projects | About ₦20,000 (airtime, printing) plus 20 founder hours | 3 qualified conversations and 1 signed project within 45 days | Fewer than 1 conversation after 10 audits | Founder | Proposed |
| E-02 | Referral partners (CAC registration agents, accountants, co-working managers) | If trusted advisers refer clients for a fee, at least 2 qualified leads arrive in 45 days | Qualified referred leads | 10% of project price, paid only when a deal closes (₦35,000 on a ₦350,000 site) | 2 qualified referrals in 45 days | 0 referrals after 5 partners approached | Founder, plus Compliance conditions | Waiting (month 2) |
| E-03 | Google Business Profile and two real case-study pages from the first clients | If the company is findable locally and shows real work, inbound enquiries start | Inbound enquiries per month | About ₦30,000 | 2 inbound enquiries per month by day 60 | 0 enquiries by day 90 | Founder | Proposed |

Budget split: E-01 ₦20,000, E-03 ₦30,000, and ₦100,000 held for E-02 referral fees (paid only on closed deals). Total ₦150,000.

## Prioritization (ICE-style scoring is in the win-clients plan)
Do E-01 and E-03 now. Start E-02 in month 2, after the referral agreement and Compliance conditions are in place.

## Funnel to measure from day 1
Prospect found, audit offered, audit done, qualified conversation, quote sent, signed, delivered, Care Plan taken. Record the reason for every lost deal.

## Data caution
With under 30 results per test, treat outcomes as directional. Do not declare a channel a winner or a failure from a handful of leads.
