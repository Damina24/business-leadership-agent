This document gives you a plan to win your first clients and become established in Abuja, the decisions we need from you, and a 90-day action plan you can start this week. # 4. What we found

1. **Care Plans are your best use of time.** Each working hour earns about ₦10,500 from a Care Plan, ₦5,083 from a Starter Website, and ₦4,000 from a Business Web App (ESTIMATE, from your prices and hours). The large custom app looks attractive but earns the least per hour.
2. **Cash, not demand, is your biggest risk.** With no sales you have about 4.8 months of cash (ESTIMATE: ₦6,000,000 divided by ₦1,250,000 a month for costs and growth spending). In our planning case you reach monthly break-even in month 9. If sales run at about half that pace, cash runs out in month 8.
3. **One custom app would use up the team.** A ₦1,800,000 app needs about 200 working hours a month for two months. Adding even one new website in the same months goes over the 256 hours we recommend planning for.
4. **Discounts cost more than they win while capacity is tight.** A 20% discount needs about 30% more sales to break even, and a 10% discount needs about 13% (ESTIMATE). You have little spare capacity to deliver extra work.
5. **The market is large and mostly not yet online.** Most small businesses use phones and WhatsApp, but few have an online store (VERIFIED, small sample). That is the gap a simple website plus a Care Plan can fill.
6. **Data protection applies from day one.** You will hold other people's personal data. You need to confirm your registration with Nigeria's data protection regulator and have a lawyer review your contract before the first signature.

## 5.2 Offer and pricing

- **Offer ladder.** Start with the Starter Website (₦350,000). Offer the Care Plan (₦50,000 a month: hosting, updates, backups, small changes) when you hand the website over. Offer a Business Web App later, once capacity and cash allow.
- **Payment terms.** 50% to start and 50% on delivery, for every project. This protects your cash (needs your approval).
- **Launch discount.** We recommend 10% (₦35,000 per client) for your first 3 clients only, in return for permission to publish a real case study (needs your approval; see Section 8).
- **Prices are not yet tested.** Your prices come from your own estimates. Record the first 5 quotes, whether each was accepted or declined, and the reason. Review prices on 2027-01-15.
- **Review costs and prices every quarter.** Inflation was 14.63% in September 2026, so hosting and tool costs can rise.

## 5.3 Winning customers

**Who to target (a starting guess to refresh after your first 3 clients).** Private schools, clinics and pharmacies, professional firms (legal, accounting, consulting), and shops or restaurants that already sell through WhatsApp. They usually have a budget of ₦300,000 to ₦2,000,000, and the owner or administrator decides.

**How to reach them.** Build a list of 30 real prospects by 2026-11-15, each with a link or note showing where you found it (Appendix B). Run three low-cost tests with clear success and stop thresholds (Appendix C):

- **Free digital audits:** offer 10 businesses a free 20-minute review of their online presence. Success is 3 qualified conversations and 1 signed project within 45 days. Stop if there are fewer than 1 conversation after 10 audits.
- **Google Business Profile and case studies:** success is 2 enquiries a month by day 60.
- **Referral partners (month 2):** success is 2 qualified referrals in 45 days.

**Outreach rules.** Write each message yourself or approve our draft before you send it. Name yourself, include a simple way to opt out, and make only claims you can prove.

# 6. Money and capacity

We built a simple month-by-month model. Half of each project is paid when you sign and half a month later. Care Plans start two months after signing, and half of your clients take one in the planning case (30% in the weak case). The first 3 websites carry the 10% launch discount. Costs are your ₦1,100,000 fixed costs and ₦150,000 growth budget every month, plus hosting and similar costs per project. The model leaves out tax, late payments, and pay rises. It shows direction and is not a forecast to bank on.

| Case | Assumptions | Lowest cash | Break-even |
|---|---|---|---|
| Planning case | 1, then 2, then 3 websites signed a month; 50% of clients take a Care Plan | ₦2,741,000 in month 8 (ESTIMATE) | Month 9 (cash in ₦1,500,000, cash out ₦1,457,000) |
| Weak case | 0, 1, 1, then 2 websites a month; 30% take a Care Plan | About ₦8,800 at the end of month 7, then below zero (ESTIMATE) | Not reached in 9 months |

**Why you cannot rely on websites alone.** Three websites a month bring about ₦915,000 of margin, and your costs are about ₦1,250,000 a month. Care Plans build up month by month and close the gap, which is why they matter so much.

**Capacity.** Your team has 320 working hours a month, and we plan with 256. In the planning case you use about 182 hours in month 3, 198 in month 6, and 216 in month 9, so you stay under the limit. One Business Web App would take about 200 hours a month for two months, which is why we advise against selling one until you have extra capacity.

| Data protection | Fines and loss of trust if you mishandle personal data | Confirm your registration tier; use a lawyer-reviewed contract with a data clause |
| Rising costs | Inflation was 14.63% in September 2026 | Review hosting and tool costs and your prices every quarter |

**Conditions to follow**

- **Outreach:** write to businesses only, record where each contact came from, name yourself, include a working opt-out, and keep a do-not-contact list.
- **Claims and testimonials:** make no "best" or results claims without proof. Use real client testimonials, with written permission.
- **Referral fees:** put the agreement in writing, and have each referrer tell the prospect about the fee.
- **Contracts:** have a lawyer review your contract before the first signature (ownership of the work, payment terms, liability, maintenance scope, and how you handle your clients' data).

**Needs a qualified professional**

- Your contract template: a Nigerian lawyer.
- Your data-protection registration tier and deadline: the regulator (ndpc.gov.ng) or a lawyer. The guidance we reviewed did not make the tier for a company like yours clear.

A middle path is to start at 10% and offer 20% only to a client who also gives a written testimonial and an introduction, with your approval.
